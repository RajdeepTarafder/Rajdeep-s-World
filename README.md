# GEOLOGY QUEST: BASIN TO BIT

**16-Bit Retro RPG Portfolio & Executive Resume** for **Rajdeep Tarafder**, Wellsite & Exploration Geologist at Oil and Natural Gas Corporation (ONGC).

Rewritten as a modern React application with TypeScript and Vite, preserving 100% of the original retro aesthetics, procedural audio synthesis, physics, mini-games, and business logic.

---

## 🎮 Features & Mini-Games

- **Mini-Game 1: Seismic Fault Sniper** (at Petrel workstation): Interactive canvas with scrolling seismic wiggle traces and moving fault/horizon targets (+250 XP).
- **Mini-Game 2: Kill the Kick!** (at BOP stack): Well control pressure gauge simulation. Keep hydrostatic pressure in the safe window for 5s to secure the 8.5" cement squeeze (+300 XP).
- **Mini-Game 3: Outcrop Hammer & Lens Roulette** (at rock outcrops): Spinning mineral scanner roulette to identify pyrope garnet, BIF, stromatolite, or helium granite (+50 XP).
- **Mini-Game 4: 24-Hour Shift Dodge** (at Coffee Doghouse): Falling drill pipes vs coffee mugs. Catch espresso mugs and dodge falling tubulars to survive the shift (+250 XP).
- **Mini-Game 5: Kimberlite Diamond ML Classifier** (at EPMA Terminal): Scatter plot canvas with Cr2O3 vs CaO axes and Dawson & Stephens G10 diamond boundary. Runs supervised ML classifier on EPMA garnet geochemistry (+150 XP).
- **Mini-Game 6: AI/SAR Satellite Despeckling Slider** (at SAR Remote Sensing Table): Interactive before/after split slider comparing raw speckled radar backscatter with AI-despeckled Sentinel-1 imagery.
- **Acrylic Fine-Art Exhibition** (at Painting Easel): Exhibition displaying original artwork, artist philosophy, and design highlights.
- **Rig Walkie-Talkie Radio Q&A** (floating 📻 button): Interactive 2-way rig radio with antenna and procedural static sound effect, answering exploration prospects, frontline ops, and academic research queries.
- **Live Milestones & Career Updater** ([U] button): Add new milestones dynamically spawning glowing pedestals on the map, view/delete milestones, and download updated `career-data.json`.
- **Boss Mode Panic Button** (🚨 BOSS MODE: STANDARD CV): ATS-optimized executive printable resume document with 1-click plain text copy and print/save to PDF.

## 🛠️ Controls

- **[W, A, S, D] / Arrow Keys:** Move player avatar
- **[H]:** Strike rock hammer
- **[M]:** Inspect with 10x hand lens
- **[F]:** Slide tackle & kick soccer ball
- **[SPACE / ENTER / E]:** Inspect workstation / interact
- **[1, 2, 3]:** Fast Travel (Archive, Petrel Lab, Rig Floor)
- **[I]:** Open Expedition Backpack items
- **[U]:** Open Live Milestones updater
- **[ESC]:** Close modals
- **Point & Click / Tap to Walk:** Tap any floor tile to walk
- **Mobile Touch Controls:** On-screen D-Pad and Action buttons
